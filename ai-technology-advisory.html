AI & Technology Advisory: identify, assess, design, implement

Practical AI opportunity identification, feasibility assessment and implementation support — aligned to a measurable business use case.

Capability overview

AI that starts with the business problem.

Good AI starts with a clear workflow and a measurable business use case — not technology for its own sake.

USR does not simply advise on AI in the abstract. Engagements are structured to move from opportunity identification through to workflow design and implementation support, so that AI becomes a working part of the business rather than a one-off report.

How we work

A four-stage approach.

01 · IDENTIFYLocate where AI can realistically add value across your workflows and data.
02 · ASSESSEvaluate feasibility, data readiness, cost and expected business impact.
03 · DESIGNDesign the workflow, tooling and controls around the specific use case.
04 · IMPLEMENTSupport build-out, testing and adoption, with clear success measures.
