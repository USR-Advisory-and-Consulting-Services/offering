Financial Services: three services, three distinct needs.

Consulting for businesses and investors, advisory for individuals and families, and practical accounting support for SMEs — each with its own purpose.

A · For businesses, investors & decision-makers

Financial Consulting

Research-driven financial consulting covering valuation, financial statements, modelling, investment research and business or investment assessment.

Valuation & Financial Statements

Assess business value and financial performance.

  • Company & business valuation
  • Financial statement analysis
  • Financial modelling

Investment & Feasibility

Evaluate opportunities using structured research and financial assessment.

  • Equity & investment research
  • Investment / opportunity assessment
  • Financial feasibility analysis

Due Diligence & Scenarios

Support transaction review and test key assumptions.

  • Financial due diligence support
  • Scenario & sensitivity analysis
  • Comparable company / benchmark analysis

Financial Consulting provides analysis and research support. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security. Business/market feasibility is covered under Strategy Consulting.

B · For individuals & families

Financial Advisory

Protection and goal-oriented financial planning for individuals and families, where applicable through the Tata AIA affiliation.

Protection Planning

Safeguarding the family’s financial position.

  • Financial protection planning
  • Life insurance planning
  • Insurance needs assessment

Goal-based Planning

Planning ahead for the goals that matter.

  • Child education planning
  • Goal-based financial planning
  • Long-term financial planning

Financial advisory and insurance-related planning services are provided through the applicable Tata AIA affiliation and are subject to applicable regulations, licensing requirements, product suitability, terms and conditions. USR is not an insurer, policy issuer, bank or financial institution, and no investment returns are promised or implied.

C · For businesses, entrepreneurs & SMEs

Accounting & Compliance Services

Practical accounting, bookkeeping and finance back-office support for businesses, entrepreneurs and SMEs.

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Maintain accurate day-to-day accounting records.

  • Accounting services
  • Bookkeeping
  • Ledger maintenance
  • Basic MIS / financial reporting

Tally & Finance Operations

Support routine finance operations and transaction processing.

  • Tally-based accounting support
  • Accounts / financial data entry
  • Invoice & expense recording
  • Accounts receivable & payable (AP/AR)
  • Bank reconciliation support

Tax & Compliance Support

Provide preparation and documentation assistance.

  • GST-related accounting & documentation support
  • ITR preparation support

Provided as accounting, documentation and preparation support. USR does not present itself as a CA firm, statutory auditor or licensed tax practitioner.
