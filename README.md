# USR Advisory & Consulting Services — GitHub Pages Website

A multi-page, static, GitHub Pages-compatible website for USR Advisory & Consulting Services.

## Final service structure
1. **Market Research & Intelligence** — market and industry research, market sizing, forecasting, competitive intelligence, customer analysis, primary/secondary research, opportunity and market-entry research.
2. **Strategy Consulting** — business/growth strategy, strategic planning, market entry/expansion, go-to-market, business diagnostics, competitive assessment, SWOT, PESTLE, Porter’s Five Forces, strategic prioritization, Balanced Scorecard and Hoshin Kanri support.
3. **Financial Advisory & Consulting** — financial and ratio analysis, valuation, DCF/multiples, peer benchmarking, financial modelling support, bookkeeping/MIS support, financial protection guidance and ITR/tax documentation support, subject to applicable requirements.
4. **Website Development & AI** — website creation/recreation, AI customer-support systems and chatbots, and practical AI tools training including prompt engineering and data analysis.

## Key positioning
**Your Partner in Strategy, Research, Finance & Digital Transformation.**

The site is positioned as a boutique independent advisory practice. Founder experience at PwC, Grant Thornton, Acuity Knowledge Partners, Aranca and Infiniti Research is clearly presented as individual professional background, not as USR clients, partners or endorsers.

## Credibility / compliance notes
- No fabricated clients, testimonials, awards, revenue figures or results are used.
- Founder employers are not represented as USR clients or partners.
- Financial services are described as analysis, valuation, business-finance support, protection guidance and documentation support; the site does not claim statutory audit, CA services or regulated securities advisory.
- The Website Development & AI vertical intentionally stays practical: website creation/recreation, customer-support chatbots and AI tools training.
