## Financial-services boundaries

- **Financial Consulting:** valuation, financial statement analysis, financial modelling, investment research, feasibility, due diligence support and scenario analysis for businesses, investors and decision-makers.
- **Financial Advisory:** protection and goal-based planning for individuals and families, including life insurance and child education planning where applicable through the Tata AIA affiliation.
- **Accounting & Compliance Services:** accounting, bookkeeping, Tally-based support, data entry, AP/AR, bank reconciliation, MIS, GST-related accounting/documentation support and ITR preparation support.

## AI Training positioning

AI Training is practical training in existing AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude for productivity, research, information analysis, data analysis, business/financial work and professional content. It is not positioned as programming, machine learning or AI development training.
