# USR Advisory & Consulting Services — GitHub Pages Website

Static, GitHub Pages-ready website for USR Advisory & Consulting Services. No build step and no application dependencies.

## Primary pages
- `index.html` — landing section / homepage
- `about.html` — About Us
- `market-intelligence.html` — Market Intelligence
- `strategy-consulting.html` — Strategy Consulting
- `financial-services.html` — Financial Consulting, Financial Advisory, Accounting & Compliance Services
- `web-development.html` — Web Development
- `ai-training.html` — AI Tools Training
- `contact.html` — Contact

## Supporting files
- `styles.css`, `script.js`
- `assets/` — logo, favicon and local editorial service visuals
- `404.html`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `CNAME`

## Business architecture
Market Intelligence → Strategy Consulting → Financial Services → Web Development, with AI Training as a separate offering.

## Publishing
Upload the repository contents to the GitHub Pages branch/root and keep `CNAME` for the custom domain.

## Content and compliance notes
No invented clients, awards, registrations, revenue, partnerships or regulatory status are presented. Tata AIA references are qualified as applicable affiliation and subject to applicable requirements. Financial Consulting is presented as research/analysis support, not as a securities recommendation. Accounting and tax content uses support/preparation-assistance wording.
