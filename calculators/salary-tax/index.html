Take-home salary planning

CTC is not the same as money received in your bank account

CTC can include employer PF, gratuity and other benefits that are not paid as monthly cash. This calculator separates those components, subtracts employee-side payroll deductions and estimates income tax.

Best practice

Use your offer letter or salary structure for the employer PF and gratuity fields. If a component is not part of your CTC, enter zero.
