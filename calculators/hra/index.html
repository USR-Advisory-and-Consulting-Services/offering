How HRA exemption is calculated

For eligible salaried taxpayers, the exemption is generally the least of: actual HRA received; rent paid minus 10% of salary (basic plus eligible DA); and 50% of salary for specified metro cities or 40% for other cities. Documentation and eligibility rules apply.
