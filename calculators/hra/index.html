HRA rules FY 2026-27

Eight cities now qualify for the 50% HRA limit

From 1 April 2026, the higher 50% salary limit applies for HRA calculations in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad. Other cities use the 40% limit. The exemption remains subject to the lowest-of-three calculation.

The three limits

1) Actual HRA received; 2) rent paid minus 10% of Basic + eligible DA; and 3) 50% of salary in the specified cities or 40% elsewhere. HRA exemption is considered here only under the old regime.

For FY 2025-26, only Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai use the 50% test. This calculator lets you switch years so older calculations are not mixed with FY 2026-27 rules.

HRA FAQs

HRA exemption questions

Which cities get the 50% HRA limit from FY 2026-27?

Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad.

Can I claim HRA exemption under the new tax regime?

No. HRA exemption is not available under the new regime.
