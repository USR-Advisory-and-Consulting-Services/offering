Automatic exchange rates

No manual exchange-rate entry

The converter fetches the latest available reference rate when you change the currencies. Reference rates can differ from the final rate offered by a bank, card network, broker or money-transfer provider because of spreads, fees and timing.

If the rate service is temporarily unavailable, the page will show an error instead of inventing a rate. The displayed rate is a reference rate, not a guaranteed executable market quote.

Currency FAQs

Currency conversion questions

Is the rate exactly what my bank will charge?

No. Banks, cards and remittance providers may add a spread or fee. Use the displayed reference rate for an estimate only.

How often are reference rates updated?

The page requests the latest available rate from its reference-rate provider whenever the selected currency pair changes.
