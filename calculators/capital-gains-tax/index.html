This calculator is limited to common listed-equity/equity-oriented mutual fund cases. It uses the post-23 July 2024 headline rates: STCG at 20% and LTCG at 12.5% above the ₹1.25 lakh annual exemption, before cess and subject to applicable conditions. Other assets, special rates, losses, grandfathering and surcharge situations can change the result.
