Compare the two tax regimes before deciding

The new regime uses the ₹4 lakh-to-₹24 lakh slab structure and ₹60,000 Section 87A rebate threshold applicable from AY 2026-27. The calculator uses a ₹75,000 new-regime standard deduction and ₹50,000 old-regime standard deduction for salary-style planning. It does not model special-rate income or complex surcharge/marginal-relief cases.

Important

HRA exemption and most Chapter VI-A deductions are generally relevant to the old regime. Verify the final position against the latest Income Tax Department rules before filing.

FAQs

Income tax calculator FAQs

Is HRA exemption available in the new regime?

No. The Income Tax Department states that HRA exemption is available under the old regime and is not available under the new regime.

Does the calculator include every tax rule?

No. It is a planning calculator for common individual income. Special-rate income, complex surcharge, capital gains, business income and other exceptions can require a separate calculation.
