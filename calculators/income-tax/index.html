AY 2026-27

Indian income tax estimate

The new regime uses the AY 2026-27 slab structure published by the Income Tax Department. The calculator includes the standard deduction assumptions and 4% health and education cess; it is intended for a common individual/salary-style estimate and does not cover every special-rate income or complex surcharge situation.

For AY 2026-27, the new-regime slabs are nil up to ₹4 lakh, followed by 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25% and 30% bands. The official Income Tax Department also states that the Section 87A rebate under the new regime is increased to ₹60,000 for total income up to ₹12 lakh, subject to the applicable rules. Check the official Income Tax Department guidance.
