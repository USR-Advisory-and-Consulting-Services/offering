About Us

A boutique advisory practice built for clearer decisions.

USR Advisory & Consulting Services is a boutique advisory and consulting firm providing research-driven, strategic, financial and technology-enabled solutions to businesses, organizations, entrepreneurs and individuals.

Who we are

Research. Strategy. Finance. Innovation.

We combine structured research with practical advice, so that recommendations rest on evidence and fit the client’s real circumstances.

Depending on the need, USR can act as an independent research partner, a strategy and finance resource for management teams, a practical accounting and finance-support provider, or a trainer helping professionals use AI tools effectively.
