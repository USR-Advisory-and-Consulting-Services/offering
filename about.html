About USR

A boutique advisory practice built for better decisions.

USR Advisory & Consulting Services brings together research, strategic thinking, business intelligence and AI-enabled solutions — helping organizations understand markets, evaluate opportunities and act with greater clarity.

Our positioning

Research is the foundation. Commercial context is the difference.

USR Advisory & Consulting Services is designed as a flexible professional-services platform for businesses, investors, entrepreneurs and consulting teams.

We combine structured research with practical advisory thinking. Depending on the assignment, USR can operate as an independent research partner, an extension of an internal strategy team, or a flexible delivery partner for consulting and professional-services firms.

The model is intentionally agile: focused projects, recurring intelligence, dedicated research capacity and specialist support can all be structured around the client's requirement.

Founder background

Built on research and consulting experience.

USR is led by a founder with 10+ years of professional experience across market research, consulting, strategy, business analysis and advisory.

This experience spans engagements at research and consulting organizations including PwC, Grant Thornton, Acuity Knowledge Partners, Aranca and Infiniti Research. These reflect the founder's individual professional background — they are not USR clients, partners or endorsers.

That background shapes how USR approaches every engagement: structured, evidence-led and focused on a decision the client actually needs to make.

Philosophy

Research is not the deliverable. The decision is.

USR treats research and analysis as inputs to a business decision, not an end product on their own.

Independent

An unaffiliated advisory practice, free to focus on what the evidence actually shows.

Structured

A defined methodology rather than open-ended exploration.

Commercially grounded

Analysis interpreted in the context of the client's market and business realities.

Right-sized

Scoped and priced to match the requirement — not a large-firm engagement model.
