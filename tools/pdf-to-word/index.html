Why use the USR Advisory tool?

This free online utility is designed around common document and image tasks: quick, simple, mobile-friendly and without a mandatory account. It is intended for everyday files and practical use.

Conversion quality

This converter focuses on clean, editable text and correct page separation. Text-heavy PDFs generally convert best. Complex layouts, scanned pages, unusual fonts, charts and positioned graphics may require OCR or a desktop converter for full visual reproduction.

Frequently asked questions

Will my PDF get an extra blank page?

The converter creates page breaks only between source PDF pages, so it does not intentionally append a trailing blank page.

Is this tool free?

Yes. There is no mandatory login or paid subscription for the tool.

Are my files uploaded?

Supported operations are designed to process files locally in your browser. External libraries may be downloaded by your browser to perform the conversion, but the selected file is not intentionally sent to USR's server.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works on modern mobile browsers, although very large PDFs may need more device memory.
