Important limitations

Browser-based conversions can differ from desktop Microsoft Office or Adobe software. Word-to-PDF and Word-to-PowerPoint use document rendering to preserve visual layout where supported; PDF-to-Word and PPT-to-Word prioritise editable text and may not reproduce complex graphics, fonts or advanced positioning exactly.
