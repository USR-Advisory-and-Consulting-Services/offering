Illustrative engagements

How USR approaches common business problems.

Actual client case studies are not published here. The examples below are illustrative scenarios showing how a research-led advisory approach would typically be structured — not descriptions of real clients, engagements or results.

These are illustrative examples for explanatory purposes only. They do not represent actual USR clients, projects, data or outcomes.
IllustrativeMarket entry

Assessing a new geographic market

A business considering expansion into a new state or country needs to understand demand, competitive intensity and route-to-market options before committing resources.

Approach

Market sizing, competitor mapping and customer-demand assessment, structured around the entry decision.

Typical output

A market-entry assessment with a recommended go/no-go view and prioritized next steps.

IllustrativeCompetitive intelligence

Responding to a new competitor

A business facing a well-funded new entrant needs a clear-eyed view of the competitive threat and where to focus its response.

Approach

Competitor benchmarking across positioning, pricing and go-to-market, combined with customer-perception input.

Typical output

A benchmarking report highlighting exposure areas and near-term response priorities.

IllustrativeAI adoption

Exploring AI for customer support

A growing business wants to reduce response times without expanding headcount, and is unsure where AI can genuinely help.

Approach

Process mapping of the current support workflow, followed by AI opportunity assessment against feasibility and impact.

Typical output

An AI opportunity map and a phased implementation plan for the highest-value use case.

IllustrativeBusiness diagnostics

Diagnosing a stalled growth trajectory

Revenue growth has flattened and the leadership team is divided on whether the cause is market, product or execution.

Approach

A structured business diagnostic combining customer feedback, competitive context and internal performance data.

Typical output

A diagnostic view identifying the primary constraint and a prioritized set of strategic responses.
