GST calculator – frequently asked questions

How do I calculate GST on an amount?

For an amount exclusive of GST, GST = taxable value × rate ÷ 100. Add GST to the taxable value to get the total. Example: ₹10,000 at 18% gives ₹1,800 GST and ₹11,800 total.

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Taxable value = inclusive amount ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). GST is the difference between the inclusive amount and taxable value. This is a reverse calculation, not simply subtracting the rate percentage from the total.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

For a qualifying intra-State supply, CGST and SGST (or UTGST) are generally levied together. For an inter-State supply, IGST generally applies. The legal place of supply and transaction facts determine the treatment.

Does my industry decide the GST rate?

No. The industry alone does not determine a GST rate. It depends on the exact goods or service classification (HSN/SAC), applicable conditions, exemptions and notifications in force. Use official rate schedules or seek professional advice where classification is unclear.

Can I use this calculator for any product or service?

You can calculate using a rate you already know, but this tool does not certify the applicable rate for a product or service. Some goods and services have special conditions, exemptions, cess or other rules.

Does this calculate my final GST payable to the government?

No. It calculates transaction-level tax amounts only. A registered business's net liability may depend on eligible input tax credit, reversals, utilisation rules, returns and other facts.

Is this GST calculator free? Do you store my amount?

Yes, it is free and requires no login. The calculations run in your browser; this page does not send the amounts you enter to a server.

Where can I verify GST rates and rules?

Check the CBIC and GST Council websites for current Acts, rate notifications and clarifications. A calculator is not a substitute for the applicable notification or professional advice.
